# Groovy

A little companion that lives at the top of your screen on Windows and Linux: he watches your AI coding agents, lets you approve and answer them, chats with the AI of your choice, and listens when you say **"Hey Groovy"**.

By **SSD**. A fork of [Coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou) by Louis Raillé (MIT License), with its own character, looks, sounds, icon and voice.

- **Download:** the newest installer is on the [Releases](../../releases) page (`Groovy-Windows-setup.exe`).
- **Everything else:** see [`windows/README.md`](windows/README.md).
