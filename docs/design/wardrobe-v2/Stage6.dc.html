Stage 6: six months together
The rare final form. It only unlocks after six months, and each look gets a special colour.
Sakura bonsai
The bonsai blossoms pink. Petals drift slowly around him, and his eyes and blush turn soft pink.
Golden flame
The whole blaze turns gold, with a white-hot core on top, gold eyes and little sparkles floating up.
Diamond sea
A golden shark, golden fish and a golden submarine take turns swimming through his sea. His eyes become diamonds and diamond flakes sparkle around him.
Timeline: Bare at hatching → stage 1 at 1 day → stage 2 at 3 days → stage 3 at 1 week → stage 4 at 2 weeks → stage 5 at 1 month → stage 6 at 6 months. In the Diamond sea, one creature swims through at a time, every few minutes, so it stays special and light on the PC.
