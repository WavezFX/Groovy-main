# Groovy body v2: design record

**4 Nov 2026, owner SSD.** Groovy's body was a rounded squircle with oval hands (Coucou-derived). The owner rejected a teardrop, pudding, dome, gem, flask, orb and lantern, then chose **Pillow + wisps (wisps 15% smaller)** after seeing options rendered by Groovy's own engine.

- `2026-11-04-options.png`: today vs wisps-only, pillow, crown dip, shoulders (Night/Ember/Frost, stages 3 and 6).
- `2026-11-04-island-and-desktop.png`: today, wisps-only, pillow, pillow with smaller wisps, collapsed and open in the island, and on the desktop.

Why: the pillow outline (sides bowed slightly in, corners pushed out) is a deliberate silhouette of Groovy's own; the wisps (leaf, flame, droplet) replace the oval hands, the closest remaining Mochi feature. Growth art, angled glowing eyes, aura and colours are unchanged. No Coucou or Mochi artwork was used or traced; all images here are Groovy renders.
